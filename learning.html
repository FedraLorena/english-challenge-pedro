Tu mapa del pasado

Simple Past: terminé → finished; fui → went; comí → ate; vi → saw.

Past Perfect: había + acción → had + participio: had finished, had gone, had eaten, had seen.

Was / were: pasado de be. I/he/she/it was; you/we/they were.

Had + objeto: tenía. Had + participio: había hecho algo.

Antes de elegir, preguntate: ¿qué ocurrió primero? Before = antes de; after = después de; when = cuando; already = ya. Before y after no obligan por sí solos a usar Past Perfect.
